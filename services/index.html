How treatment works

  1. 01

    Understanding the severity

    We look into the severity of the condition first and then understand how medication, CBT, DBT, TMS or bio feedback can be beneficial in your case. We understand in what intensity everything is to be provided and adjust it according to the time and the progress shown by the client.

  2. 02

    The first phase

    When it comes to chronic illness and addiction, understanding and soothing, the acute phases is very important. The after-care plans on follow up continue well into the future to appoint where the crisis has already passed, but continuous treatment plan is the only option to maintain recovery.

  3. 03

    Family involvement

    When it comes to conditions that are treated in the long-term, having a support system that understands you best and knows your true nature is very essential to have by your side. Your family support is a part of the care and the team works with families in a very continuous manner to provide psycho education to them.
