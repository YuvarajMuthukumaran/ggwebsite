About Dr. Gorav Gupta

A practice built around the illnesses that take the longest to heal.

Dr. Gorav Gupta is a senior psychiatrist in Delhi NCR, in practice since 1997. His clinical interest sits where psychiatry is hardest and slowest: de-addiction medicine, long-term rehabilitation, and dual diagnosis, where a substance use disorder runs alongside another psychiatric illness.

He works through a team of psychiatrists, psychologists, addiction counsellors and paramedical staff rather than alone, across four hospitals in Delhi, Gurugram, Noida and Mehrauli.

Biography

Dr. Gorav Gupta, Senior Consultant Psychiatrist of the Indian Sub Continent, practices in Delhi NCR. He completed his MBBS from the prestigious institute, Maulana Azad Medical College (Delhi University) and M.D. from Lady Hardinge Medical College (Delhi University) in 1997. He is renowned for pioneering super specialized work in psychosocial rehabilitation in psychiatry.

Tulasi Psychiatric & Rehabilitation Centre

Tulasi Psychiatric & Rehabilitation Centre is a psychiatric hospital and rehabilitation centre, where patients with addiction, psychosis, agitated and violent behavior are treated. The Psychiatric Hospital is designed to handle all kinds of psychiatric emergencies and is licensed by State Mental Health Authority. Treatment is carried out under the able guidance of Dr Gupta. His mental health team comprises of psychiatrists, psychologists, addiction counselors, and other medical and paramedical staff. Dr Gupta has a keen interest in the treatment of dual diagnosis that is substance abuse disorder with psychiatric co-morbidity. Other doctors and psychiatrist frequently refer their patients to him. He is well known for his clinical acumen and behavior therapy. He has not only treated patients from India but also from countries like U.K., Canada, Nepal, Bhutan, and Afghanistan.

Tulasi Home

Tulasi Home is a registered NGO, a rehabilitation facility for Schizophrenia, Bipolar Disorder, and other Chronic Mental Diseases recognized under PWD ACT 1995. The treatment span for patients generally varies from 3-6 months depending on the kind of illness. The team here also comprises of psychiatrists, psychologists, social workers, and medical and paramedical staff. It can accommodate 45 patients at a given time. The objective of rehabilitation is to make this patient functional so as to integrate them into society. Over the years, this place has been recognized for its invaluable contribution to the field of rehabilitation of chronic mental illness. Many institutions refer their patients for cost-effective and good rehabilitation programs to Tulasi Home. We provide services at a pan-India level.

Community initiatives

The NGO also runs fortnightly camps for the underprivileged people, and a small tuition center is run for the underprivileged kids by the patients within the premises, this not only keeps our patients busy but helps in instilling confidence in them. The Centre also runs outreach community programs for patients, a designated team of psychologists and a nursing staff is sent to the patient’s home to check the progress of the patient.
