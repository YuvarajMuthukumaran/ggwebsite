About Dr. Gorav Gupta

A well renowned name in the field of psychiatry in Delhi NCR, his expertise has been honed for 40+ years guiding clients in their de-addiction and rehabilitation journey.

Know your expert

Dr. Gorav Gupta is an established senior consultant psychologist and the Founder of Tulasi Healthcare, Delhi. His deep experience within the field of de-addiction and rehabilitation led him to understand the concerns faced by his patients and led him to establish an institution that caters to their needs in the most compassionate and ethical way possible.

Pioneering in specialised work in psychosocial rehabilitation in psychiatry, his expertise has been honed by delivery on multiple national projects and being a part of the most prestigious societies and institutions that govern the psychiatric space in the country.

His expertise extends to areas of geriatric psychiatry, pharmacological management, community outreach services, occupational therapy, as well as forensic psychiatry.

Biography

Graduating from some of the top institutions that cater to psychiatric education in the country, Dr. Gorav Gupta became a senior consultant psychiatrist after he pursued his M.B.B.S. from Maulana Azad Medical College and his M.D. from Lady Hardinge Medical College in 1997.

After he honed his skills deeply in the field of rehabilitation and de-addiction psychiatry, he decided to step into the community through Tulasi Psychiatric & Rehabilitation Centre. Successfully running for 15 years, it is a well-known institution that has adopted multiple modern treatment modules to treat addiction and many other mental health related disorders in the most effective ways possible.

Serving the community, he has been running various charitable activities like free OPD camps for the underprivileged and working with the Delhi government in the Department of Social Welfare to understand the concerns of the society and providing manageable measures to take care of the same.

Tulasi Psychiatric & Rehabilitation Centre ↗

Tulasi Psychiatric & Rehabilitation Centre is an institution designed for treating mental health illness along with their psychiatric concerns. From the most simple to severe cases, we have experts equipped with different insights to tackle any kind of concern possible. We receive patients with addiction, psychosis, agitated and violent behaviour under emergency care as well. Licensed by the State Mental Health Authority, treatment is carried out under Dr. Gupta’s guidance. Our team comprises psychiatrists, psychologists, counsellors and other medical and paramedical staff. Due to a keen interest in dual diagnosis and substance disorders, Dr. Gupta specialises in these concerns, garnering reference from many experts in the country. He is very well known for his clinical insights and behavioural therapy. Individuals from Canada, UK, Nepal, Bhutan, and Afghanistan have approached him for treatment after receiving glorious referrals from different experienced individuals.

Tulasi Home ↗

Tulasi Home is a registered NGO that caters to rehabilitation facilities provided for schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, and other mental health conditions that are chronic in nature. The home is recognised under the PWD Act, 1995. Since it is a long-term care home, individuals reside here for around 3 to 6 months, depending on the kind of illness they are facing. The team here, as well, is composed of psychiatrists, psychologists and other paramedical staff. Currently, it has a capacity of 45 patients. With the goal of rehabilitating patients back into the society, the home has been recognised for its contribution in the field of rehabilitated practices.

Community initiatives

The NGO is also involved in organising fortnightly camps that cater to underprivileged people who are looking for mental healthcare, but do not have access to the resources. The Centre is also involved in community programmes where a designated team is sent to specific spaces to check the progress of the patients registered there.
