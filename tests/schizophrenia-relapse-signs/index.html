Schizophrenia Relapse Symptom Monitoring Scale

A simplified rating scale for 10 important symptoms of a schizophrenia relapse, to help individuals and caregivers monitor changes over time.

  • 10 questions
  • About 3 min
  • Private

What this test measures

A simplified rating scale for the 10 important symptoms of a schizophrenia relapse can help individuals and caregivers monitor changes over time. This scale is designed for general monitoring and should not replace professional medical advice.

How to answer

For each symptom listed below, rate the occurrence over the past week by choosing the option that best describes the current situation. It can be filled in for yourself or by a caregiver for someone else.

  • 0Not at all
  • 1Mild Present but manageable; slight impact on daily life
  • 2Moderate Definitely present; moderate impact on daily life and functioning
  • 3Severe Very disruptive; significantly impacts daily life and functioning

Private by design: your answers are worked out in your browser only. Nothing is saved, sent or shared.

This is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. Only a qualified professional can assess and diagnose a mental health condition.
