Bipolar Disorder Early Warning Sign Scale

Track the frequency and intensity of certain behaviors and feelings over time, to recognise patterns worth discussing with a healthcare professional.

  • 11 questions
  • About 3 min
  • Private

What this test measures

This form can be used to track the frequency and intensity of certain behaviors and feelings over time. It’s important to note that this is not a diagnostic tool, but rather a way to recognize patterns that may warrant a discussion with a healthcare professional.

How to answer

For each item, rate the frequency and intensity over the past week on a scale from 0 to 4.

  • 0Not at all
  • 1Rarely
  • 2Sometimes
  • 3Often
  • 4Very Often/Intensely

Private by design: your answers are worked out in your browser only. Nothing is saved, sent or shared.

This is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. Only a qualified professional can assess and diagnose a mental health condition.
