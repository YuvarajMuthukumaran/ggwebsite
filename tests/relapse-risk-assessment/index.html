Relapse Risk Assessment Scale

A self-assessment of early relapse warning signs for people in recovery from substance use, to help in early identification and prompt intervention.

  • 16 questions
  • About 4 min
  • Private

What this test measures

Assessing the risk of relapse in individuals with Substance Use Disorder (SUD) can help in early identification and prompt intervention. This tool can be used by individuals in recovery or by clinicians as part of a therapeutic process.

How to answer

Please read each statement below and rate how much it has applied to you in the past week on a scale from 0 to 4.

  • 0Not at all
  • 1Rarely
  • 2Sometimes
  • 3Often
  • 4Very Often

Private by design: your answers are worked out in your browser only. Nothing is saved, sent or shared.

This is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. Only a qualified professional can assess and diagnose a mental health condition.
