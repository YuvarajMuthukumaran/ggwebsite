Substance Use Differentiation Questionnaire

A screening questionnaire to help understand your substance use patterns and whether a professional assessment is advisable.

  • 12 questions
  • About 2 min
  • Private

What this test measures

This questionnaire is a tool to help understand your substance use patterns and may indicate if a professional assessment is advisable. It is not exhaustive or diagnostic but can help identify potential red flags that warrant a more thorough assessment.

How to answer

Please answer the following questions honestly.

  • Yes
  • No

For example alcohol, cannabis or tobacco. This stays on your device and is only used to word the questions.

Private by design: your answers are worked out in your browser only. Nothing is saved, sent or shared.

This is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. Only a qualified professional can assess and diagnose a mental health condition.
