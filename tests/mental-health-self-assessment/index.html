Mental Health Self-Assessment Scale

This tool can help an individual reflect on their mental health and behaviors.

  • 10 questions
  • About 3 min
  • Private

What this test measures

This tool can help an individual reflect on their mental health and behaviors. This mental health assessment questionnaire helps evaluate a person’s overall mental health.

How to answer

Each question can be answered on a scale from 0 to 4. This scale is for self-reflection or a general assessment and is not a substitute for professional diagnosis or evaluation.

  • 0Not at all
  • 1Rarely
  • 2Sometimes
  • 3Often
  • 4Very Often

Private by design: your answers are worked out in your browser only. Nothing is saved, sent or shared.

This is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. Only a qualified professional can assess and diagnose a mental health condition.
