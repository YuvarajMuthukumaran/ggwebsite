Interactive tests

Online screening is one of the fastest & easiest ways to determine whether you are experiencing symptoms of a mental health condition. After mental health testing, you can consult with Dr. Gorav Gupta.

  • 01

    Mental Health Self-Assessment Scale

    This tool can help an individual reflect on their mental health and behaviors.

    • 10 questions
    • About 3 min
    • Private
    Start test →
  • 02

    Bipolar Disorder Early Warning Sign Scale

    Track the frequency and intensity of certain behaviors and feelings over time, to recognise patterns worth discussing with a healthcare professional.

    • 11 questions
    • About 3 min
    • Private
    Start test →
  • 03

    Schizophrenia Relapse Symptom Monitoring Scale

    A simplified rating scale for 10 important symptoms of a schizophrenia relapse, to help individuals and caregivers monitor changes over time.

    • 10 questions
    • About 3 min
    • Private
    Start test →
  • 04

    Relapse Risk Assessment Scale

    A self-assessment of early relapse warning signs for people in recovery from substance use, to help in early identification and prompt intervention.

    • 16 questions
    • About 4 min
    • Private
    Start test →
  • 05

    Substance Use Differentiation Questionnaire

    A screening questionnaire to help understand your substance use patterns and whether a professional assessment is advisable.

    • 12 questions
    • About 2 min
    • Private
    Start test →

Private by design: your answers are worked out in your browser only. Nothing is saved, sent or shared.

This is a screening tool, not a diagnosis. Only a qualified professional can assess and diagnose a mental health condition.
