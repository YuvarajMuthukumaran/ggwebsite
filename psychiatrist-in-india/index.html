Why consider visiting a psychiatrist?

There are certain times you might feel concerned, overwhelmed or just constantly tired facing issues, responsibilities and daily chores. It is quite normal. Burnout is a common feeling, but if it occurs on a regular basis and seems to stay, it can become a matter of concern. There can be many factors at play that might be preventing full recovery and blocking ways to feel positivity. For this reason, a psychiatrist can help you with-

Make a formal diagnosis- Sometimes, we just over analyze a situation. Getting a formal diagnosis is a way to ensure that there are actually no problems with oneself and that we are taking care of ourselves very well. According to some of the top psychiatrists in India, it is essential to consult once in while to ensure your mind’s wellbeing.

Early intervention- As we all know, prevention is better than cure. If caregivers manage to find an issue at the right time, early interventions can help them curb the situation and prevent them from fully blossoming into a severe disorder or chronic condition.

Understand physical contributors- Sometimes, there are physical factors that can contribute to the development of mental health conditions. For example, if a person has an amputation, they may develop depression and anxiety about their future. Or if a person has a deficiency of Vitamin D, that can also lead to development of depression and anxiety. In this manner, physical factors highly contribute to mental health problems, which can only be correlated and understood with the help of a professional.

Receive comprehensive support- Even if you were not diagnosed with any problem does not mean that you were not under stress and anxiety. This is what leads you to a professional. As a caregiver, we understand the need of providing support even when it is not asked for. Providing a bubble of support and understanding with some easy-to-follow tips and guides may be the only help a client needs to blossom back into their healthy and true self.

Connecting for long-term support- Long-term support is very essential when a person recovers from a severe condition. With our facility, every client receives the best resources and high-end aftercare to keep in touch with their value base and renew their treatment regimes.

Do psychiatrists function differently?

Psychiatrists are similar to any other mental health practitioner, but they have a medical degree that helps them understand the different perspectives of diagnosis and treatment.
So, they actually function in many different ways and roles along with some mental health practitioners of the field to build a treatment regime that is applicable, easy to administer and personalized according to the client’s needs.

With this as their goal, a psychiatrist employs and works with psychologists, psychiatric nurses, social workers, counselors, group facilitators and holistic activity planners to gain insights from every perspective and plan the best treatment possible.

When evidence-based and holistic approaches are combined, it brings an amalgamation of comprehensive treatments whose efficacy is on par with the top treatment plans.

What symptoms should I look for?

There are certain symptoms that can become your cue for visiting a psychiatrist. Remember to note and assess these symptoms for their regularity and frequency so as to inform your caregiver and provide them with the best medical history as possible. This way, you can track your condition as well as gain insights into your problem. You can take a quick mental health test today.

– Problems in communicating and understanding one’s own emotions
– Feeling anger and rage without cause and having frequent outbursts
– Feeling empty, lonely and sad
– Severed interpersonal connections and feeling distant from close people
– Inability to concentrate and function on a daily basis
– Feeling that the daily chores are also a heavy task
– Experiencing the need to escape your daily life and shunning your responsibilities.
– Experiencing suicidal thoughts and attempting to self-harm
– Issues with substance use
– Declining interest in social issues and distancing from social circle
– History of trauma and abuse that feels to be resurfacing
– Declining occupational tasks
– Feeling an unnecessary emotional, mental, and cognitive burden that does not have any cause

What issues do psychiatrists deal with?

There are a plethora of mental, physical and emotional issues that lie in the spectrum of psychiatric concern. With the help of our mental health experts, we have devised care and treatment regimes that will be able to identify the right needs and induce rapid transformation in the client’s condition. The issues psychiatrists deal with include-
Depression- Depression deflects our energy and focus, and is a condition that affects millions of people worldwide. It is not easy to deal with depression, given the interplaying factors, circumstances and the issues with which a person is dealing. With a world class psychiatric evaluation from the best psychiatrists in India, you can feel the difference when the recovery happens and you are surrounded by the best resources and support that a rehab center can offer.

Anxiety- We all feel agitated and nervous, but if it escalates to the point of no return, it is understandable to approach an expert. Anxiety can be induced for many different reasons, come from different sources and have different impacts on the person’s life. This can lead to many types of disorders, which can be tackled with the help of an expert.

Stress and related problems- Stress is a relatively common condition, given we all suffer from it at some point in our lives. When the stress escalates to the point where it can induce symptoms of psychological distress is where it requires an intervention. With a team of experts at your disposal, you can gain keen insights into your condition, consult for guidance and draw up an easy to follow plan to control your stress.

Personality disorders- Personality disorders are a cognitive, emotional and thought disturbance issue that changes the behavioral pattern of the individual and renders them out of touch from their own surroundings. It is not easy to diagnose and recognize the development of personality disorders, let alone provide early interventions for the same. With good guidance and thorough diagnosis, people can actually manage and control their symptoms on time with expert care.

Addiction and related issues- Addiction has been burring its holes into the society’s roots for a long time, but its effects can be truly felt with the exponentially rising cases of addiction and its related disorders. Substances like marijuana, cocaine, tobacco, methamphetamines and alcohol have a severe impact on the functioning of the body and mind. To tackle this, a strenuous rehab program is essential to get rid of all the substances from the body and set on a journey to transformation. As one of the best psychiatrist in India, Dr. Gorav Gupta is known for his expertise in the field of rehabilitation psychiatry.

Schizophrenia- Schizophrenia is a cognitive and behavioral disturbance that is accompanied by the presence of hallucinations and delusions. This causes problems in daily functioning of an individual and renders them dependent on their caregivers. Since schizophrenia shares its origins from adolescence, recognizing its symptoms at an early stage can be a challenge. With the best psychiatrist for schizophrenia on our team, we are proud to provide the best care regime possible.

Dementia- Dementia is a chronic condition that is accompanied with memory loss, loss of insight and disturbed orientation. It requires a lot of care and supervision to tackle a person with dementia, and it is not truly possible to care for them without an expert hand. At our facility, we provide a chronic care home to manage the symptoms, supervise and provide appropriate treatment to people with dementia and other chronic disorders.

How many therapy sessions do you need?

The number of therapy sessions needed varies by individual. Some people benefit from just a few sessions, while others may need months or years. It depends on your goals, issues, and progress On an average, sessions last from 5-20 in number. Your psychiatrist can help assess what’s best for you.
