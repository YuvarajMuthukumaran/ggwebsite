  3. 03

    Necessities to bring to the consultation

  4. 04

    We look into the history of assessment, and a treatment plan which is truly matching the severity of your condition. If it helps, structured assessment tools are applied alongside having a conversation to understand the objectivity around your concern.

Points to consider before coming in

How do I know whether I should see a psychiatrist?

If you are suffering from some incomprehensible mental health situation where you are experiencing more issues, anxiety, compulsion, or any substance use disorder that are getting in your way of life, consider seeing a psychiatrist in Delhi. You do not need to be certain if something is wrong with you.

What happens at the first consultation?

At the first consultation, our experts try to take your history, provide an assessment and take an objective approach towards treatment. A treatment plan is then recognised, which is laid down in front of you to help you understand what you can expect during the journey.

What does treatment involve?

A treatment plan can involve different kinds of therapeutic modalities like CBT, DBT, dTMS, biofeedback or medications if required. This treatment is delivered by a team of experts rather than a single individual to help you tackle different kinds of concerns effectively.

What happens after the first phase of treatment?

After the first phase, there is an acute phase where having the right instructions and the right guidance matters most. Aftercare and follow-up plans continue well after the treatment ends.

Can I consult online?

Yes, you can definitely consult online as well as in person with our experts.

Can family members be involved?

Definitely. We encourage family members to be involved in the process throughout so that their loved ones understand and gain strength from their words. For long-term and chronic issues, it is definitely advised.
