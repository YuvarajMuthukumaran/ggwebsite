1. 01
    Foundation years

    Integrating mind and medicine

    Dr. Gupta pursued his M.B.B.S. from Maulana Azad Medical College (MAMC), Delhi, and his M.D. in Psychiatry from Lady Hardinge Medical College, Delhi. With strong foundations, he stepped into the world of clinical psychiatry and dedicated his early years to learning and building his expertise.

    • M.B.B.S., MAMC Delhi
    • M.D. Psychiatry, Lady Hardinge Medical College
  2. 02
    Specialisation

    Understanding de-addiction and dual diagnosis

    He developed a special affinity towards addiction medicine and rehabilitation for people with substance use, understanding the criticality of psychiatric illness and substance use occurring together. As one of the most difficult branches of clinical psychiatry, his work focused on bringing long-term change and a quick, proper diagnosis to every person.

    Recognition and accolades
    • Delhi Psychiatry Society
    • Indian Association of Private Psychiatry
    • Indian Association of Psychiatry
  3. 03
    Beginning of Tulasi Healthcare

    Care built from experience

    Dr. Gupta established Tulasi Psychiatric & Rehabilitation Centre, a facility catering to addiction, psychiatric and psychological conditions. He also established Tulasi Home, a registered NGO working on long-term rehabilitation. The organisations are recognised under the PWD Act, 1995.

    • Tulasi Psychiatric & Rehabilitation Centre ↗
    • Tulasi Home, registered NGO ↗
  4. 04
    Working beyond the need

    Giving back to society

    Organising a free OPD and regular camps for people who cannot approach or otherwise afford regular care for their mental health, he works with the Delhi Government’s Department of Social Welfare to understand and give back to society in his own way.

    Recognition and accolades
    • Panel member, Mental Healthcare Act 2017
    • Economic Times “Psychiatrist of the Year” award
    • Member, Department of Social Welfare, Govt. of Delhi
    • Co-opted member, NITI Aayog National Task Force on Brain Health
