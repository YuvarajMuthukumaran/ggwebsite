BIPOLAR DISORDER- SIGNS & SYMPTOMS

Bipolar disorder or maniac depressive illness is a disorder related to the brain in which the person experiences unusual mood swings, euphoria as they feel highly energetic bustling with energy with high activity levels. The condition is characterized by-

  1. Mood and Behavioral Changes: It includes euphoria, impulsiveness, pressured sleep, being easily distracted, and restlessness. Mood changes may also include depression, anxiety, irritability, violence, suicidal tendency, hostility etc. Some behavioral changes may include alcohol abuse, increased goal directed activities.
  2. Psychotic Symptoms: Delusions and hallucinations are some psychotic symptoms of bipolar disorders.
  3. Cognitive Symptoms: Racing thoughts, high distractibility, disorganization, and inattentiveness are some common symptoms.

The symptoms can vary from moderate to severe and might influence the person’s job/academic performance. Suicidal tendencies, relationship problems are common among people suffering from bipolar disorder. However, the good news is that it can be treated and people suffering from the same can lead a normal life.

TREATMENTS AND SOLUTIONS

Being a form of mental illness, Bipolar disorder requires immediate treatment. Once, the family members or any close acquaintances identifies the symptoms, a diagnosis should be conducted under the supervision of the best doctor for mental illness in Delhi. Medication and psychotherapy are the two main solutions to treat bipolar disorder. Only prescribed medications should be taken to ensure effectiveness and great results.

Prescribed Medications

Mood stabilizing medications like Lithium, valproic acid or divalproex sodium, and Lamotrigine etc. are great for stabilizing the fluctuating mood. Atypical antipsychotic medications are also good for stabilizing the person affected by bipolar disorder. Some of the highly recommended medicines are Olanzapine, Aripiprazole, and Quetiapine.

Psychotherapy

There are different types of psychotherapies that can positively affect the person and reduce the effect of disorders to a great extent. Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Family-focused therapy, Interpersonal and social rhythm therapy, Electroconvulsive Therapy (ECT) are ideal solutions to this problem.
