The matter of the fact is- Yes. Therapy does work.

Talk therapy or psychotherapy is a great tool in itself to manage most issues. Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), for example, helps people reframe negative thoughts, while other methods like Interpersonal Therapy (IPT) focus on improving relationships and communication. It may sound pretty straightforward, but it does work its wonders!

The Journal of the American Medical Association revealed that psychological treatments have a significant impact in resolving major depressive disorder, sometimes even more effective than medications. The Lancet Psychiatry supported this finding, suggesting that CBT leads to moderate to high impacts in symptoms.
