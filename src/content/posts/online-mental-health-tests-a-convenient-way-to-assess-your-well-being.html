What exactly are Online Mental health tests?

These are self assessment tools that are designed to help an individual evaluate their mental health in the comfort of their homes. They often consist of a series of questions that assess different facets of mental health, including mood, stress levels, anxiety, depression, and overall emotional evaluation.
