Understanding Cannabis Addiction

Cannabis addiction, clinically known as Cannabis Use Disorder (CUD), is a mental health condition characterized by the compulsive and problematic use of cannabis despite negative consequences. These consequences can include impaired cognitive function, strained relationships, legal issues, and declining physical and mental health.

Signs of cannabis addiction may include:

  • Increased Tolerance: Needing more cannabis to achieve the desired effects.
  • Withdrawal Symptoms: Experiencing symptoms like irritability, insomnia, and loss of appetite when attempting to quit.
  • Neglected Responsibilities: Failing to meet work, school, or family obligations due to cannabis use.
  • Failed Attempts to Quit: Repeatedly trying to stop or cut down on cannabis use without success.
  • Loss of Interest: Diminished interest in activities that were once enjoyable.
