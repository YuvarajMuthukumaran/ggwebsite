When to seek a psychiatrist? Seeking a psychiatrist's help is advisable when you are experiencing significant mental health challenges that are interfering with your daily life and well-being. Here are some specific situations in which you should consider seeking a psychiatrist: Persistent and overwhelming feelings of sadness, anxiety, or despair. Thoughts of self-harm or suicide. Severe mood swings or uncontrollable anger. Difficulty concentrating or making decisions. Hallucinations or delusions. Extreme stress or trauma.
What is mental illness? Mental illness, also known as mental health disorder or psychiatric disorder, refers to a wide range of conditions that affect a person's thinking, emotions, mood, behavior, or the combination of these aspects. These conditions can significantly impact a person's thoughts, feelings, and daily life functioning.
Can psychiatrist prescribe medication? Yes, psychiatrists are medical doctors who specialize in the diagnosis and treatment of mental health conditions. They are authorized to prescribe medication as part of their treatment approach.
