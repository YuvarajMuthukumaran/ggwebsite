How often should you see a psychiatrist?

In the beginning, depending upon severity, clients can make bi-weekly or tri-weekly visits. The frequency of visit however, is decided by the psychiatrist themselves according to the client’s need and availability.

As treatment progresses, your psychiatrist might suggest stretching out the intervals to once a month or even less frequently, depending on your stability and goals.

The frequency of progression can look somewhat like this-

  • Initial phase– bi-weekly or tri-weekly visits
  • Stabilization phase– every 2-3 weeks for a clear followup and assessment
  • Maintenance phase– Monthly or as prescribed by the psychiatrist

What’s the difference between a psychiatrist and a psychologist?

A psychiatrist is a medical professional that assesses mental health conditions and prescribes medication management. Psychologists on the other hand, focus on therapy, counseling, and behavioral support. They, however, cannot prescribe medication without a psychiatrist’s consent. If you feel unsure which one to approach, going to a psychiatrist in Gurgaon can help you get a proper assessment and, if needed, a referral to a therapist for continued emotional support.
