About Dr. Gorav Gupta

A name in the field of psychiatry in Delhi NCR, his expertise has been honed for {{exp}} years guiding clients in their de-addiction and rehabilitation journey.

Know your expert

Dr. Gorav Gupta is an established senior consultant psychiatrist and the Founder of Tulasi Healthcare, Delhi. His deep experience within the field of de-addiction and rehabilitation led him to understand the concerns faced by his patients and led him to establish an institution that caters to their needs in the most compassionate and ethical way possible.

Pioneering in specialised work in psychosocial rehabilitation in psychiatry, his expertise has been honed by delivery on multiple national projects and being a part of the most prestigious societies and institutions that govern the psychiatric space in the country.

His expertise extends to areas of geriatric psychiatry, pharmacological management, community outreach services, occupational therapy, as well as forensic psychiatry.

Biography

Graduating from some of the top institutions that cater to psychiatric education in the country, Dr. Gorav Gupta became a senior consultant psychiatrist after he pursued his M.B.B.S. from Maulana Azad Medical College and his M.D. from Lady Hardinge Medical College in {{since}}.

After he honed his skills deeply in the field of rehabilitation and de-addiction psychiatry, he decided to step into the community through Tulasi Psychiatric & Rehabilitation Centre. Running since 2002, it is a well-known institution that has adopted multiple modern treatment modules to treat addiction and many other mental health related disorders in the most effective ways possible.

Serving the community, he has been running various charitable activities like free OPD camps for the underprivileged and working with the Delhi government in the Department of Social Welfare to understand the concerns of the society and providing manageable measures to take care of the same.

How Dr. Gorav Gupta works

The same approach at every consulting room and online.

The first consultation

It starts with listening: what has been happening, for how long, and how it affects work, sleep, relationships and daily life. An assessment and a diagnosis follow, then a plan that is explained in plain words before anything begins.

When admission is needed

Most people are treated as outpatients. Admission is for when someone cannot be kept safe or cannot be treated at home, and it is decided after an assessment, together with the family. How OPD and admission differ.

How families are involved

Families are often the first to notice and the ones who make the call. With the patient’s agreement, they are told about the plan, what to watch for, and what to do if symptoms worsen or use starts again.

Tulasi Psychiatric & Rehabilitation Centre ↗

Treats mental health illness from the simplest to the most severe, including emergency care for addiction, psychosis and agitated behaviour, under Dr. Gupta’s guidance.

  • State Mental Health Authority licence
Read more

Tulasi Psychiatric & Rehabilitation Centre is an institution designed for treating mental health illness along with their psychiatric concerns. From the most simple to severe cases, we have experts equipped with different insights to tackle any kind of concern possible. We receive patients with addiction, psychosis, agitated and violent behaviour under emergency care as well. Licensed by the State Mental Health Authority, treatment is carried out under Dr. Gupta’s guidance. Our team comprises psychiatrists, psychologists, counsellors and other medical and paramedical staff. Due to a keen interest in dual diagnosis and substance disorders, Dr. Gupta specialises in these concerns, garnering reference from many experts in the country. He is very well known for his clinical insights and behavioural therapy. Individuals from Canada, UK, Nepal, Bhutan, and Afghanistan have approached him for treatment after receiving referrals from different experienced individuals.

Tulasi Home ↗

A registered NGO providing long-term residential rehabilitation for schizophrenia, bipolar disorder and other chronic mental health conditions.

  • 45-patient capacity
  • 3–6 month stays
  • {{rpwd_act}}
Read more

Tulasi Home is a registered NGO that caters to rehabilitation facilities provided for schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, and other mental health conditions that are chronic in nature. The home is recognised under the {{rpwd_act}}. Since it is a long-term care home, individuals reside here for around 3 to 6 months, depending on the kind of illness they are facing. The team here, as well, is composed of psychiatrists, psychologists and other paramedical staff. Currently, it has a capacity of 45 patients. With the goal of rehabilitating patients back into the society, the home has been recognised for its contribution in the field of rehabilitated practices.
