Common questions

What to know before you come in

How do I know whether I should see a psychiatrist?

If you are under stress for a considerable amount of time and feel that you are experiencing low mood, anxiety and struggles around your relationships, it is suggested that you consider seeing a psychiatrist in Delhi. You do not need to decide whether it is really a problem first: a consultation is meant for exactly that.

What happens at the first consultation?

Our experts gather history around what you are experiencing and understand what you are going through. After proper information gathering, we apply tools and assessments that help us take the conversation forward and build a structured treatment plan around your concerns.

What does treatment involve?

The treatment plan involves a mix of therapy, assessments and, if required by the severity of your condition, medication management. The treatment is delivered by a team of experts rather than just one person, who can help you understand different parts of your concerns.

What happens in the first phase of treatment?

There is a separate phase for each acute and chronic illness. In acute illness, symptom management is the first phase; in chronic conditions, stabilising the concerns and the first three months after it matter most. We provide review and rehabilitation on a continuous basis even after the crisis has passed.

Can I consult online?

Yes. Consultations are available with Dr. Gupta in online mode as well.

Can family members be involved?

Yes. We encourage family members to be a part of the supportive care, especially when a chronic condition is involved.
