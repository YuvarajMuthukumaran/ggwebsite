Schizophrenia

A chronic illness that destabilises thoughts and perceptions. With the help of evidence-based treatment plans, we focus on reducing symptoms that distress you and provide psychological interventions tailored to your goals.

Bipolar disorder

Depression

With everyday implications, depression can make life difficult, affecting energy and concentration. Evaluating symptoms and medical history, we work on combining psychological treatment with psychotherapy wherever appropriate.

Anxiety and OCD

Common questions

What to know before you come in

What does a consultation cost, and when is Dr. Gorav Gupta available?

A consultation is {{fee}} for {{session}} minutes. OPD runs in New Delhi on {{opd_delhi}}, and online on {{opd_online}}. Times shift around ward rounds and admissions, so the team confirms your slot when they call back. Ask for a call back.

How do I know whether I should see a psychiatrist?

If you are under stress for a considerable amount of time and feel that you are experiencing low mood, anxiety and struggles around your relationships, it is suggested that you consider seeing a psychiatrist in Delhi. You don’t need to consider if it really needs to be a problem first – a consultation is meant for it.

What happens at the first consultation?

Our experts tend to gather history around what you are experiencing and understand what you’re going through. After a proper resource gathering, we work on applying tools and assessments that help us take the conversation forward and build a structured treatment plan around your concerns.

What does treatment involve?

The treatment plan involves a mix of therapy, assessments, and medication management if required in case of severity of your condition. The treatment is delivered by a team of experts rather than just one person who can help you understand different parts of your concerns.

What happens in the first phase of treatment?

There is a separate phase for each acute and chronic illness. In the acute illnesses, symptom management is the first phase and in the chronic conditions, stabilising the concerns and the first three months after it matter most. We work on providing review and rehabilitation on a continuous basis even after the crisis has passed.

Can I consult online?

Yes. Consultations are available with Dr. Gupta on online mode as well.

Can family members be involved?

Yes. We encourage family members to be a part of the supportive care, especially when a chronic condition is involved.
