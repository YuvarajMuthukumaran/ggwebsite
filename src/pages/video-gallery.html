Welcome to our video resources page. Dr. Gorav Gupta is a senior psychiatrist in Delhi, NCR & Gurgaon. At Dr. Gorav Gupta YouTube channel, we believe in providing comprehensive mental health care to our patients. To complement our in-person and telehealth services, we have created a collection of informative and supportive videos to assist you on your mental health journey. Our goal is to empower you with knowledge, offer guidance, and reduce the stigma associated with mental health. Explore our video library to find valuable information and expert insights on different psychiatric disorders like Bipolar, Schizophrenia, Personality disorders, OCD, Depression, Dementia, and Addiction disorders like drug addiction, alcohol addiction, and marijuana addiction.
