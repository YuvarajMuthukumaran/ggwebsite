After you get in touch

What happens next?

  1. 1

    Our team connects with you

    An expert from Dr. Gupta‘s team will look into how you are doing, give you the information around OPD hours and understand how you can find a time to consult with him.

  2. 2

    You choose where

    It’s up to you to understand that your comfort lies in your own hands. We offer both online and in person consultations. If your family member is the one who is trying to seek help, you can come along to support them.

  3. 3

    Necessities to bring to the consultation

    There are some crucial information resources that we require before we start any kind of treatment. Earlier prescriptions, discharge summaries or test reports are very important. If you have none of that or any kind of history around things, come in, anyway.

  4. 4

    The first consultation

    We look into the history of assessment, and treatment plan which is truly matching the severity of your condition. If it helps, structured assessment tools are applied alongside having a conversation to understand the objectivity around your concern.

Points to consider before coming in

How do I know whether I should see a psychiatrist?

If you are suffering from some incomprehensible mental health situation where you are experiencing more issues, anxiety, compulsion, or any substance use disorder that are getting in your way of life, consider seeing a psychiatrist in Delhi. You do not need to be certain if something is wrong with you.

What happens at the first consultation?

At first consultation, are experts. Try to take your history, provide an assessment and take an objective approach towards treatment. A treatment plan is then recognised, which is laid down in front of you to help you understand what you can expect during the journey.

What does treatment involve?

A treatment plan can involve different kinds of therapeutic modalities like CBT, DBT, DTMS, bio feedback or medications if required. This treatment is delivered by a team of experts rather than a single individual to help you tackle different kinds of concerns effectively.

What happens after the first phase of treatment?

After the first phase, there is an acute phase where having the right instructions and the right guidance matters most. After care and follow up plans continue well after the treatment ends.

Can I consult online?

Yes, you can definitely consult online as well as in person with our experts.

Can family members be involved?

Definitely. We encourage family members to be involved in the process throughout so that their loved ones understand and gain strength from their words. For long-term and chronic issues, it is definitely advised.
