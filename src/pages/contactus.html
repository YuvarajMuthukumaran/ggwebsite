After you get in touch

What happens next

  1. 01

    The team calls you back

    Someone from Dr. Gupta’s team calls during OPD hours to understand what is going on and find a time. The call comes from a Delhi number.

  2. 02

    You choose where

    In person at either clinic, or online. If a family member is the one seeking help, they can make the appointment and come along.

  3. 03

    What to bring

    Any earlier prescriptions, discharge summaries or test reports, and the names and doses of anything being taken now. If you have none of that, come anyway.

  4. 04

    The first consultation

    A full history and assessment, and a treatment plan matched to severity. Structured assessment tools are used alongside the conversation where they help.

Booking and visit questions

Still unsure? Call the clinic. The team is happy to talk it through.

Do I need a referral to see Dr. Gupta?

No. You can book directly by calling {{phone}} or using the callback form on this page.

Which clinic should I visit, Delhi or Gurugram?

Choose whichever is closer for regular OPD visits. The Gurugram hospital also provides acute and inpatient care if that is needed.

Can I consult Dr. Gupta online?

Yes. Online video consultations run on {{opd_online}}. The team sends the link when they call back.

How do I know whether I should see a psychiatrist?

If low mood, anxiety, compulsions, substance use or changes in thinking are getting in the way of work, relationships or sleep for more than a few weeks, an assessment is reasonable. You do not need to be certain what is wrong.

What happens at a first consultation?

Dr. Gupta takes a detailed history, asks about your symptoms and their impact, and discusses options with you. You leave with a clear plan for next steps.

Can a family member book on someone’s behalf?

Yes. Many appointments are arranged by family members, who are welcome to come along to the consultation.
