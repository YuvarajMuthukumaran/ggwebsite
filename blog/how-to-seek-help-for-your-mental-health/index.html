How to seek help for your mental health: Moving past the stigma

21 November 2024

We’re living in a world of struggles and constant judgment. Ideas and words float around us- and we catch them without thinking twice. We attach ourselves to words that do not mean anything- and carry them with us to the point where they become toxic to our well-being. Most of us carry this hurt- which manifests as mental health disorders to addiction disorders. Most of the issues that we encounter during our treatment regime have stemmed from small issues. Manifestations come in a variety of manners. These manifestations cannot be controlled and show up in the daily functioning and habits of the individual. These habits grow into maladaptive behaviors, and show up as drug use, drinking habits, non-normative behaviors and symptomatic behaviors for various mental health conditions. Our facility is a haven of self-transformation and prime health, which is evident from our results across the years. With the best team gathered from some of the premiere institutions and trained in various techniques of physiotherapy and rehabilitation, let’s explore how we can encourage you to seek help and move past the stigma of receiving psychiatric treatment.

Why seek psychiatric treatment? What problems can it solve?

Psychiatric treatment is the helm of maintaining a good mental health regime. It is not only about the factors that affect us on a daily basis, it is also about the problems that we downplay to carry on with our lives. These problems can be as small as not being able to cope with daily tasks, to feeling depressed due to familial issues. Each one of us carry these problems with us everyday and they are bound to take their toll on us one day or the other. The taboo that surrounds us makes it hard for many to take a helping hand and suffer in silence for the times to come. This is why, today we discuss the problems that a psychiatric intervention can solve and raise awareness amongst our readers.

Tacking addiction disorder- Addiction disorder is one the highest reported psychiatric issues around the globe. Only a small percentage of substance and addiction disorder clients receive treatment in India. Only 1 in 38 people receive treatment or are aware about it. With such a large treatment gap, it is our duty as the top psychiatrist in Delhi to raise awareness on the topic and help as many individuals as we can to get their lives back on track. With a 12 step rehabilitation program and a dedicated team of addiction abuse experts on our panel, a personalized approach is our strength in resolving addiction disorders.

Severe psychiatric disorders- When it comes to identifying severe psychiatric disorders, we are all about vigilance and care. Issues like Dementia, Alzheimer’s and Parkinsons take a huge toll on an individual and their family. With a chronic care home in place, experts can monitor the client’s health and provide them with a daily functioning regime that would not have been possible in the care of their family members.

Stress, Anxiety and Depression- The problems of stress, anxiety and depression are highly correlated and complex in their own way. Most people with psychiatric issues report these problems as a symptom of daily occurrence, or note that their problems started with them. At our mental hospital, we have noted a daily reporting of these symptoms and devised the best methods for their management and treatment. Weather
Stress, anxiety or depression are the chief complaints or a result of an underlying psychiatric issue, the most effective treatment plans are available with the top psychiatrist in Delhi.

Personality disorders- personality disorders are highly complex psychiatric disorders that affect the daily functioning and even the quality of interpersonal relationships that a client has. They can be hard to diagnose, since a lot of intricacies are involved and may not be recognised by the family members. Till the time these symptoms begin to show, the problem usually escalates towards expert intervention. With the top psychiatrist in Delhi for personality disorders, you can get the right guidance early on.

Obsessive Compulsive Disorder-OCD is one of the disorders with best symptomatic presentations. It can be categorized as a mental disorder which requires proper psychiatric interventions to alleviate the problem. It is usually characterized by obsessive thoughts that are irrational in nature coupled with compulsive behavior that makes their daily life a nonsensical affair. As per some of the top psychiatrist in Delhi, one can identify these symptoms only with a keen observation which can include-
– Noticing repetitive behaviors
– Spending majority of time on these ‘repetitive tasks’
– Their lives are significantly entangled with such thoughts and this affects their daily necessary activities.

These are the major issues that people face. Many other critical disorders are also related to these manifestations and can only be recognized when a psychiatrist provides their opinion on them. To know more about such disorders, you can visit Best Psychiatrist in Delhi today..
