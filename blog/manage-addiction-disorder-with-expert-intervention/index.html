How to spot if your loved one is falling into an addiction disorder?

There are a number of symptoms to consider when you can consider potential addiction disorder brewing. As a top psychiatrist in Delhi, Dr. Gorav Gupta highlights some signs to look for before you approach an expert for intervention. Let’s take a look what an addiction disorder looks like-

  • Uses the substance regularly, without fail and several times a day
  • Feeling an intense urge that surpasses any important task at hand
  • The use of the substance grows day by day
  • Making sure that the substance remains with you at all times, even when the conditions are not certain and appropriate
  • Going financially overboard to secure the substance, no matter how it affects the person and their family
  • Neglecting social, occupational and familial responsibilities because the substance overpowers this sense of responsibility.
  • Engaging in risky activities despite knowing they can be fatal.
  • Spending large amounts of time using the substance and then recovering from its effects.
  • Noticing symptoms of withdrawal like restlessness, anxiety, depression and physical issues while not consuming the substance.
  • Getting aggressive towards small issues
  • Shifting personality characters and not feeling like self under the influence of the substance.

Best treatment options for addiction disorder.

When you feel that most of the statements mentioned above are true for your loved ones, it is time to consult an expert. Treatment options are available under both inpatient and outpatient categories. Let’s see what addiction disorder treatment options can one pursue according to their condition-

Inpatient management- For most cases, an inpatient treatment plan is more of a necessity than a choice. Since addicts are more prone to behave out of their impulse, it is necessary to monitor them 24/7 and keep a track of their condition and improvements over time. This not only helps them stay away from the substance, but helps them to contemplate, and truly focus on their treatment.

Medication therapy- Medications are used to reduce the urges, cravings and manage withdrawal symptoms. With withdrawal symptoms, it is hard to administer any therapy and approach a rehabilitation program. According to the top psychiatrist in Delhi for counseling, it is necessary that all the elements work together to bring the best results.

Rehabilitation- With a 12 step rehabilitation process, an individual is made to live a life of ‘sobriety’ and ‘resilience’. Here, therapies, holistic living along with a daily routine are set up to manage addiction in the long term and live a healthier life.

Therapeutic intervention- Therapeutic intervention is a necessary step to gain perspective and change maladaptive behavior. It dwells into the root causes of the problem that become the prerogative for the illnesses to come. Addiction disorders can be best treated with the help of therapies like Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (CBT), Psychotherapy (talk therapy), Dialectical Behavior Therapy, depending upon the case and the severity that the client presents.

Group Therapy- Group therapy is an intervention applied in the later stages of therapy that is all about relating to the people who are in the same journey as them and gaining insight on how to own and manage their problems. Sometimes, a person may feel alone and think they are the only one suffering, but that is not the case. With group therapy, they can relate to other individuals of the same mindset and share their perils and wins.It acts as a process of catharsis.

Family Therapy- Family therapy is a necessary step to truly resolve issues of addiction. Since not only one person is affected with it, a family unit suffers the brunt of the issues when an individual is ridden with addiction disorder. This way, the client and their family can indulge and discuss their situation and resolve conflicts together.
