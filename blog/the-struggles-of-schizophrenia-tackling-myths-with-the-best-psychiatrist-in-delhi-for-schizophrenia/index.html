The struggles of Schizophrenia: Tackling myths with the best psychiatrist in delhi for schizophrenia

22 February 2025

Schizophrenia can be explained as one of the most highly taxing conditions that a person can encounter. Without much awareness and support in its regard, it can become a major mental health condition for which interventions may be a challenge.

As the best psychiatrist in Delhi, Dr. Gorav Gupta has encountered many cases of different magnitudes. Clients are haunted by the possibilities of supernatural occurrences, but do not even stop and consider the occurrence of a mental illness. By the time a consultation happens, early intervention is a far cry.

Many caregivers can be heard saying- ‘he has gone mad. Talks to himself and creates ruckus all the time.’

‘He is afraid of things that are not there- must be something supernatural’

‘He is definitely struck with a curse. Please help him’

Now, these sentences are common to a psychiatrist, but depict the sad, dire state of the Indian mental health landscape. Without further adieu, let’s explore some common myths as encountered and heard by the best psychiatrist in Delhi for schizophrenia, Dr. Gorav Gupta.

If you have heard such myths or need to clear ones related to the disease, visiting our schizophrenia rehabilitation center in Noida can work in your favor.

Myth 1- People with Schizophrenia are violent & dangerous

Reality– Most of the people who are affected with Schizophrenia are more likely to be victims of violence rather than being the perpetrator. Even if such a behavior is expressed, it might only be a result of underlying conditions like substance abuse or inflicted indifference. Confusion, disorientation and lack of support can also trigger this state. With the symptoms in control, the clients start to regain their sense of self.

It is important to realize that they are experiencing a state of mental illness that cannot be understood by others. So, providing exceptional support is a principle followed by Dr. Gorav Gupta. As the best psychiatrist in Delhi, he upholds his values to educate and fight for the right and humane treatment of mental health clients.

Myth 2- Schizophrenia reduces the ability of a person to function at all.

Reality- As the best psychiatrist in Delhi for schizophrenia, it can be assured from our end that it is not at all true! A person who is struggling with schizophrenia may face some roadblocks on the way, but it is definitely something that can be overcome with the right treatment. With the right therapy, counseling and treatment support, a person can return to their normal lives and maintain a social structure. Moreover, many people with schizophrenia are highly capable of achieving personal goals and contributing meaningfully to society. It’s essential to provide people with schizophrenia the opportunity and resources to reach their full potential, rather than assuming they cannot succeed due to their diagnosis.

Myth 3- Schizophrenia only happens to Elders

Reality- It may seem like it, since the issues of old age seem to be somewhat similar, but it is not true. Schizophrenia usually starts to manifest from the ages of 16-30, and the symptoms can be observed. It is hard to pin the symptoms directly to Schizophrenia to some extent. But it only rarely shows up in elderly individuals. Since this mentality persits, younger individuals experiencing symptoms do not get access to proper care. If timely intervention is provided, severity of the problem can be controlled and managed. As the best psychiatrist in Delhi for Schizophrenia, Dr. Gorav Gupta has built resources that help clients aid themselves and connect with experts.

Myth 4- Only medication can heal Schizophrenia

Reality- As per our experts, the answer is a clear no. Schizophrenia is a mental health condition. It does not only affect the body but the mind with it. To curb its effects and provide a long term management strategy for it, therapy, counseling and medication need to be provided equally. An effective treatment plan typically involves- a holistic approach that includes medication, psychotherapy, and social support. Cognitive-behavioral therapy (CBT) and other therapeutic interventions can help individuals with schizophrenia learn coping strategies, manage stress, and address the negative impact of the disorder. In addition, our schizophrenia rehabilitation center in Noida offers family therapy and peer support groups that can provide essential emotional support.

Myth 5- Schizophrenia is a result of childhood traumas and bad upbringing

Reality- While situational factors may form a part of development of Schizophrenia, they are not always the root cause. According to the best psychiatrist in Delhi with an experience in examining different types of cases, finding the root cause of such issues is a difficult task. Along with this, it holds genetic and behavioral factors that also contribute to the onset

Environmental factors like stress, drug abuse, or prenatal exposure to certain viruses or durgs can also play a role in triggering the condition. Childhood trauma and negative life events may increase the risk of developing schizophrenia in people who are genetically predisposed, but they are not the direct cause of the disorder.

Myth 6- Schizophrenia is caused by supernatural entities

Reality- This myth is the basis of many issues that develop as a result of effective interventions and consultations. From the experience of a psychiatrist who is well versed in handling such cases, it sounds like the talks of ancient times.

Schizophrenia is a mental health condition, and not the work of a supernatural entity. People who are not aware of mental health disorders harbor certain beliefs about such conditions and consider it as the work of a demon or the devil himself.

But we can assure you, it is not the case.
