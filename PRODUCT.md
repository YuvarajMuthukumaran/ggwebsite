# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Primary: patients and, above all, their families in and around Delhi NCR (often a worried relative) looking for a psychiatrist or for de-addiction and long-term rehabilitation care. They arrive anxious, often from a search for "psychiatrist in Delhi / Gurugram", on a phone, and want to know quickly whether this doctor treats their situation, whether he is credible, and how to book or call.
Secondary: referring doctors and institutions, who refer patients to Tulasi.

## Product Purpose
The personal website of Dr. Gorav Gupta, a senior psychiatrist (M.B.B.S., M.D. Psychiatry) who treats addiction, dual diagnosis, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder, depression, anxiety and OCD, and who guides care at Tulasi Healthcare (Tulasi Psychiatric & Rehabilitation Centre and Tulasi Home). Success is a visitor who trusts him enough to call, WhatsApp or request a time, and who finds reliable information on conditions along the way.

## Positioning
A single senior psychiatrist whose practice centres on the illnesses that take longest to treat (de-addiction, rehabilitation, dual diagnosis), backed by his own acute centre and long-term rehabilitation home, with a team approach.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Pages: home (single long page), About, Journey, Services, Resources (blog, FAQ, videos, publications, testimonials), Contact, plus ~26 imported condition pages and ~98 blog posts.
- Experience figure is "40+ years" (client-confirmed). The M.D. is dated 1997.
- Do not invent medical claims, credentials, testimonials or statistics; reuse the doctor's own copy.
- Some copy conflicts are open and flagged in README (capacity of Tulasi Home, camp frequency, superlatives such as "best", a film banner that shows a different phone number and "since 2002").

## Brand Commitments
Navy and white with a blue accent, Geist type, the Tulasi Healthcare logo (hands holding a brain), the existing portrait photography. The client explicitly dislikes dark boxes for the booking block, heavy white space, card-heavy layouts, and a hero line other than "Best doctor in Delhi NCR".

## Evidence on Hand
Google reviews (six on the home page), press links with the doctor's quotes, eight press/YouTube items, short films of the doctor speaking (assets/video), photographs (assets/img), imported articles. No awards imagery or case studies beyond these; do not fabricate more.

## Product Principles
1. Trust before polish: calm, legible, clinical-professional; never salesy.
2. A distressed visitor can reach a phone or WhatsApp in one tap from anywhere.
3. Say less, more clearly: compact layouts, no dead space, one consistent type scale.
4. Every claim traces to the doctor's own material.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Readable at phone width; sufficient contrast; keyboard-reachable controls; respects reduced motion. Hindi content appears in places (films, press), so Devanagari must render properly.
